Aaple Sarkar & DigiLocker Step-by-Step Guide

Prepared for: Homemakers, Students & Residents of Palghar District

1. Setting Up DigiLocker on Your Smartphone

Key Law: Documents issued in DigiLocker (Aadhaar, Driving Licence, Vehicle RC, Marksheets) are 100% legally valid across India under Rule 9A of the Information Technology Rules, 2016.

Step-by-step setup:

  1. Download DigiLocker from Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
  2. Tap Get Started > Create Account.
  3. Enter your full name as printed on your Aadhaar Card, Date of Birth, and your registered mobile number.
  4. Set a 6-digit security PIN.
  5. Enter the Aadhaar number and verify with the OTP sent to your registered SIM.
  6. Your documents are now safely stored and can be shown to traffic police or college authorities without carrying physical originals!

2. Navigating Aaple Sarkar (Maharashtra Govt Services Portal)

Website: aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in

  • Services available: Income Certificate (Tahsil Office), Domicile & Nationality Certificate, Caste Certificate, Senior Citizen Card, Ration Card modification.
  • Requirement before applying:
    • Recent passport size photograph (Size between 5KB to 20KB).
    • Proof of Identity (Aadhaar Card / Voter ID).
    • Proof of Address (Electricity bill / Ration card).
