Safe UPI & Mobile Payments Handbook

Prepared for: Factory Workers (MIDC), Small Vendors & Residents of Boisar

1. What You Need to Know Before Scanning a QR Code

Step 1: Check the Display Name
When you scan a shop's QR code on PhonePe, Google Pay, or Paytm, always glance at the verified merchant name displayed on the screen before proceeding.
Step 2: Verification of Payment Success
Do not just rely on the verbal speaker sound. Check the green checkmark with the transaction ID on your phone screen.

2. Five Strict Rules of Digital Banking

  1. Never share your UPI PIN: It works just like your ATM card secret code.
  2. No PIN for Receiving: If someone claims they are sending you money and asks you to enter your PIN, cancel the transaction immediately.
  3. Set Transaction Limits: You can set daily UPI transaction limits in your banking app (e.g. ₹5,000 per day) to avoid high financial risks.
  4. Review Transaction History Weekly: Check your bank passbook or statement for any unfamiliar debits.
  5. Keep Your Screen Private: In crowded places like Boisar railway station or MIDC market, shield your screen while entering your PIN.

3. If You Face a Wrong Debit or Failed Payment

1. Open your UPI App > Tap the specific transaction in History > Tap "Have an issue? / Raise Dispute".

2. Note down the 12-digit UTR Number.

3. If unauthorized transfer occurred, call your bank customer care immediately and dial 1930 (National Cybercrime Reporting).
