Cyber-Safety & Scam Avoidance Checklist

Prepared by: Dept. of Information Technology, Theem College of Arts, Commerce & Science, Boisar

🚨 Cyber Fraud Emergency? Call 1930 immediately or visit cybercrime.gov.in within the first hour!

1. Electricity Bill Disconnection Scam (Most Common in Boisar & MIDC)

Sample Fake Message: "Dear consumer, your electricity power will be cut tonight at 9:30 PM from MSEDCL office because your last month bill was not updated. Call electricity officer at 98765-XXXXX immediately."

How to protect yourself:

  • Mahavitaran (MSEDCL) never sends SMS messages from private personal 10-digit mobile numbers. Official SMS headers are typically VM-MSEDCL or VK-MSEDCL.
  • Never call phone numbers provided inside SMS text messages.
  • Check your genuine electricity bill status only by opening the official Mahavitaran Consumer Portal app or visiting your local Boisar West electricity billing center.

2. The Golden Rule of UPI and Mobile Payments

  • Your UPI PIN is entered ONLY to SEND money out of your account.
  • Receiving money (salaries, lottery prizes, refunds, Olx payments) requires NO PIN. If someone asks you to enter a PIN to receive cash, it is a 100% scam.
  • Never scan any QR code sent to you over WhatsApp to "claim a prize".

3. Dangerous APK Downloads & Screen Sharing

  • Never install files with the extension .apk sent via WhatsApp or Telegram claiming to be updates for banking, lottery, or courier packages.
  • Never install remote control apps like AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or QuickSupport at the request of anyone claiming to be from customer care or your bank.

4. Where to Get Free Local Help in Boisar

Visit the Assisted E-Service Help Desk at Theem College Campus, Room 204. Trained student mentors will check your phone settings, remove malicious apps, and configure privacy controls free of charge.
