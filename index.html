About the Source Academic Study

Community Engagement Project: Digital Literacy Impact Assessment Using Data Science — A Study of Boisar, Palghar District

1. Research Background & Context

Boisar is a major industrial and semi-urban center within Palghar district of Maharashtra, hosting the MIDC industrial belt alongside residential, agricultural, and tribal communities. While smartphone proliferation is high across households, formal digital capability—such as filing government forms, navigating Aaple Sarkar, or recognizing phishing—remains uneven.

2. Academic Origin & Project Team

This initiative and website were formulated under the academic guidance of the Department of Information Technology, Theem College of Arts, Commerce & Science, Boisar.

  • Student Researchers: Rohit Kumar Prasad (Roll No. 17) · Sunny Singh (Roll No. 25)
  • Project Mentor: Prof. Sarvesh K Kasar
  • Academic Program: B.Sc. Data Science / Information Technology
  • Publication Version: 1.0 (Draft for Review — September 2026)

3. Ethical Guidelines & Data Privacy (Section 3.8)

In accordance with research ethics standards, this platform practices strict privacy-by-design:

  • No personal identifiable information (names, mobile numbers, Aadhaar numbers, residential addresses) is collected or stored.
  • Under no circumstances are banking credentials, UPI PINs, passwords, or OTPs ever requested.
  • All Self-Check entries generate anonymous token codes (e.g. DLB-xxxx) ensuring individuals can re-measure their progress while preserving aggregate confidentiality.
